# Lasting modifiers

What 2024 spells change on a creature while they last (defenses, speed, Advantage, roll bonuses, Armor Class) and how the effect components of stage 7 in [plan/effects.md](../plan/effects.md) store it. Researched on 2026-10-16.

## Rule

- **Advantage / Disadvantage:** roll two d20s and take the higher / lower. A roll can't have more than one of each; Advantage and Disadvantage on the same roll cancel.
- **D20 Test** covers ability checks, attack rolls and saving throws: whatever affects D20 Tests affects all three.
- **Resistance** halves damage of that type (rounded down), once per instance. **Vulnerability** doubles it. **Immunity** to a damage type or a condition means it doesn't affect the creature.
- **Speed:** «If an effect increases or decreases your Speed for a time, any special speed you have increases or decreases by an equal amount for the same duration»: the glossary doesn't say how «doubled» or «halved» carries over; the app applies the same operation to every special speed (decided with the user on 2026-10-16: Fly 60 under Haste is 120, Speed 0 makes every special speed 0; see [combining-effects.md](combining-effects.md)). Halving rounds down («Round Down»). Special speeds are Fly, Climb, Swim and Burrow; with several speeds the creature picks one per move and may switch, subtracting what it has already moved. Grappled, Paralyzed, Petrified and Restrained: «Your Speed is 0 and can't increase». The glossary has no rule for combining several Speed changes.
- **Armor Class:** the base is 10 + Dexterity modifier. A rule may give another base calculation; the creature uses only one of them.

## How the app uses it

Decided with the user on 2026-10-16: not one universal «modifier» component but six kinds with their own fields, built one at a time ([plan/effects.md](../plan/effects.md), stage 7).

- **7.1 «Сопротивление и иммунитет»** (done): a level (Сопротивление, Уязвимость, Иммунитет) and the damage types it covers; Immunity may also list conditions. One component may cover several types, like Stoneskin. In one effect a damage type or a condition appears once per level; different levels for one type aren't forbidden, how they combine is a rule of play on the map. The Resistance cantrip's 1d4 isn't this component: it stays in the description.
- **7.2 «Запрет лечения»** (done): no fields, the bearer can't regain Hit Points, as from Chill Touch. Temporary Hit Points still come: getting them isn't healing ([per-turn-damage.md](per-turn-damage.md), unverified wording). Once per effect; the form disables the menu item when the effect already has it.
- **7.3 «Скорость»** (done): one change from a list. Ordinary Speed: «Больше на N фт.», «Меньше на N фт.» (N is always positive: a separate item instead of a minus sign, which phone keypads often lack), «Вдвое больше», «Вдвое меньше», «Становится 0». A special speed: Fly, Climb, Swim or Burrow, either N ft. or «равна Скорости» (Spider Climb); Fly may also hover. N is a multiple of 5 up to 300. Decided with the user on 2026-10-16, since the book doesn't say how several changes combine: one change of the ordinary Speed per effect, each special speed once. No fetched spell grants a Burrow Speed; it's in the list because the glossary defines it. Ray of Frost's −10 ft. lasts «until the start of your next turn», which is the effect duration «До следующего хода», not part of this component. Haste's Speed 0 after it ends is a separate effect. That a change of Speed also changes the special speeds is a rule of play on the map; the form only says so.
- **7.4 «Преимущество или помеха»** (done): Advantage or Disadvantage on one kind of roll: own attack rolls, attack rolls against the bearer, saving throws, ability checks, or all D20 Tests (Foresight). Saves and checks take one ability or all of them. Strength checks and saves (Enlarge/Reduce) or checks and saves with one ability (Bestow Curse) are two components. The same rolls appear once per effect, whether with Advantage or Disadvantage: by the book the two cancel on one roll, so both in one effect mean nothing; «all saves» and «Strength saves» are different rolls and may go together. Decided alone: one option was clearly simplest and covers every spell above.
  - **Condition «Когда»** (added 2026-10-16, gap А of [plan/effects-coverage.md](../plan/effects-coverage.md)): «always» or one condition from a short list that depends on the roll, so that the engine can check it on the map. Attacks against the bearer: the attacker is within 5 ft. or farther (Prone: «An attack roll against you has Advantage if the attacker is within 5 feet of you. Otherwise, that attack roll has Disadvantage»), the attacker can see the bearer (Faerie Fire: «Attack rolls against an affected creature or object have Advantage if the attacker can see it»). Own attacks: only against the one who applied the effect (Bestow Curse: «The target has Disadvantage on attack rolls against you»), against anyone but them (Grappled: «Disadvantage on attack rolls against any target other than the grappler»). Own attacks, saves, checks, D20 Tests: while the one who applied it is in the bearer's line of sight (Frightened: «while the source of fear is within line of sight»). The applier becomes known to the engine only with the ability → effect link (gap К), until then these are data. The same rolls with different conditions aren't a repeat: Prone is two components. Stored records without the field read as «always». Decided alone: the list covers every condition and spell read, and a free-text condition couldn't be checked.
  - **Initiative** (added 2026-10-16, gap Е): a roll of its own, without an ability or a condition. Invisible: «If you're Invisible when you roll Initiative, you have Advantage on the roll»; Incapacitated: Disadvantage on Initiative. The glossary: «If you have Advantage on Initiative rolls, increase your Initiative score by 5».
  - **Left in the description:** a skill or a purpose («Perception to find it», Hunter's Mark; Lightly Obscured), «only the next attack» (Guiding Bolt: approximated by the duration «До следующего хода» if wanted), attacks only by certain creature types (Protection from Evil and Good: the app has no creature types), Blur's «immune if it perceives you with Blindsight or Truesight», the charmer's Advantage on social checks (Charmed: a roll of the charmer, not of the bearer). Invisible gives its Advantage and Disadvantage through the condition component (`CONDITION_RULES`, [conditions.md](conditions.md)); its «unless the creature can see you» isn't expressible yet: gap Р of [plan/effects-coverage.md](../plan/effects-coverage.md).
- **«Автопровал»** (done 2026-10-16, gap Б of [plan/effects-coverage.md](../plan/effects-coverage.md); not one of the six stage 7 kinds): the bearer automatically fails saving throws (one ability or all), ability checks that need sight, or ability checks that need hearing. Strength and Dexterity saves (Paralyzed and three more conditions) are two components, like Advantage. The same rolls appear once per effect. In the 2024 books only conditions do this, so the component is for homebrew effects and for the engine, which will build the conditions from the same parts. Automatic success (Truesight against illusions, the spells' own saves) isn't a component: it belongs to a save, not to a creature. Decided alone: the three rolls are everything the glossary lists.
- 7.5–7.6 (no roll bonus and AC components yet): see the plan.
- **Left in the description:** protections that depend on who attacks (Protection from Evil and Good), Haste's extra action, Death Ward, Freedom of Movement's «can't reduce Speed». Speed changes inside an area (Spirit Guardians) and Difficult Terrain (Entangle, Web, Grease, Spike Growth) aren't this component: they belong to the area on the map. Expeditious Retreat and Jump don't change Speed.
